# Wizard 201 — Repair stage

## What it is

**Mission mode:** process drift, inconsistent artifacts, or a blocked stage.

**Parent:** [Wizard 201 — Mission modes](10-wizard-201.md).

## When to use it

When recheck or review keeps surfacing the same class of gap and you need a corrective posture.

## Outcome

A corrective run plan and recheck you can track to closure.

## Short example

| Starting situation | What “good” looks like after the session |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Recheck keeps failing on the same gap | Fewer open inconsistencies; recheck passes or gaps are explicitly accepted |
